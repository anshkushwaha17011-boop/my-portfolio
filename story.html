Our Journey

June 2023 — 2026

June 2023

The First Meeting

Hum pehli baar tuition mein mile. Woh Class 6 mein thi aur main Class 7 mein.

Shuru mein sab kuch bilkul normal tha. Mujhe uske liye kuch romantic feel nahi hota tha.

Phir dheere-dheere tuition ke baad friends ke saath time spend karna, games khelna aur activities karna hua… aur pata hi nahi chala kab mujhe uski taraf attraction hone laga.

July 2023

The One-Sided Beginning

Mere bhai ne mujhe uska Instagram bataya. Maine follow kiya, lekin shuru mein usne follow back nahi kiya aur na hi reply kiya.

Mujhe pata tha ki jo main feel karta hoon, woh shayad woh feel nahi karti.

Ek din woh tuition aayi aur bina kuch kahe chali gayi. Maine Instagram par message kiya. Uska reply tha, “bas aise hi.”

2023 ke baaki months mein feelings one-sided hi rahi. Tuition mein thodi baat hoti thi aur Instagram par kabhi reply milta, kabhi nahi.

2024

A Feeling That Stayed

June 2024 mein maine woh tuition chhod di aur doosri tuition join kar li. Hamari baat almost band ho gayi.

Main uski Instagram stories aur posts dekhta tha, lekin kabhi confess nahi kiya. Dar tha ki jo chhoti si friendship hai, woh bhi khatam na ho jaye.

2024 khatam ho gaya, lekin meri feelings nahi. Mujhe usmein kuch aisa dikhta tha jo mujhe kisi aur mein nahi dikhta tha.

17 January 2025

Her Birthday

Uske birthday par maine Instagram story lagayi aur wish kiya. Sab kuch normal tha.

23 January 2025

Yahin Se Sab Kuch Badal Gaya

Meri younger sister ka birthday tha. Family celebration ke baad raat ko main Instagram par reels dekh raha tha aur maine use online dekha.

Maine message kiya. Us raat hum lagbhag 2 ghante baat karte rahe — pehli baar itni long conversation hui.

Uske baad lagbhag 5–6 din har week baat hone lagi. Yeh around 20 din chala.

Phir ek din usne kaha, “main baat nahi karungi, mujhe text mat karna.”

Maine reason poocha, lekin reply nahi aaya. Maine message karna band kar diya.

March 2025

The Conversation Returned

Lagbhag ek mahine tak baat nahi hui. Phir maine ek normal reel bheji. Usne reply kiya aur conversation dobara start ho gayi.

Uske baad hum roz kam se kam 2 ghante baat karne lage.

Holi 2025

The Day I Met Her Again

Holi ki evening main apni mother ke saath unki friend ke ghar gaya tha. Main usse normally baat kar raha tha.

Usne kaha ki use kahin jaana hai aur baad mein baat karegi. Phir mujhe pata chala ki woh aur uski mother aur siblings bhi usi jagah aaye hue the.

Woh moment mere liye unforgettable tha.

Maine use side mein bulaya, usse face-to-face colour lagaya aur Holi wish ki.

March 2025

The Answer I Had Waited For

Holi ke around ek week baad, baat karte hue maine finally usse propose kiya.

Main bahut dar raha tha. Phir bhi maine apni feelings keh di.

Usne YES kaha.

20 March 2025 — jis din meri one-sided feelings officially ek relationship ban gayi.

Main bahut khush tha. Jis person ko main itne time se chahta tha, usne bhi mujhe choose kiya.

March — June 2025

Three Beautiful Months

Relationship ke first three months bahut acche rahe. Woh mujhe pyaar karti thi aur main usse bahut pyaar karta tha.

Woh time mere liye genuinely bahut special tha.

July 2025

When Family Found Out

July mein family ko hamare baare mein pata chala. Mere ghar par problems hui, lekin eventually situation settle ho gayi.

Uske baad bhi relationship normal raha aur hum theek the.

October 2025

The First Serious Fight

Chhath ke time main Jharkhand mein tha aur woh yahan thi. Hamari pehli serious fight hui.

Usne kaha ki woh relationship mein nahi rehna chahti. Gusse mein maine bhi keh diya, “thik hai, jao jo mann kare.”

Baad mein maine use samjhaya aur manane ki koshish ki. Eventually things became okay again.

1 January 2026

A New Year, Another Fight

New Year ke din phir fight hui. Usne dobara kaha ki woh relationship mein nahi rehna chahti.

Main emotional ho gaya tha. Main nahi chahta tha ki meri wajah se woh sad ho. Eventually things became okay again.

2026

Her Birthday

Uske birthday par bhi hamari fight hui. Lekin next day sab theek ho gaya.

February 2026

Another Set of Memories

Uske maternal uncle ki wedding thi. Shopping, preparations aur wedding ke beech relationship normal tha.

February mein ek personal memory bhi bani jo meri life ke important moments mein se ek hai.

March — April 2026

A Calm Phase

March aur April relatively normal aur acche rahe. April mein ek small family problem aayi, lekin kuch serious nahi hua.

May 2026

The Longest Silence

May mein ek bahut serious fight hui. Mujhe laga maine kuch galat nahi kiya tha, lekin situation suddenly badal gayi.

Lagbhag 15 din tak baat nahi hui. Mujhe laga shayad sab khatam ho gaya.

Phir uska message aaya aur things became okay again.

May — June 2026

One More Good Day

Reconcile hone ke next day uske school ka water-park trip tha.

School ke baad main use pick karne gaya. Uske baad humne thoda city explore kiya aur phir woh apne ghar chali gayi.

June 2026 bahut achha gaya.

July 2026

When Everything Changed

July ki shuruaat bahut achhi thi. Sab kuch normal tha aur mujhe bilkul idea nahi tha ki month ka ending itna difficult hoga.

July ke ending mein meri wajah se uske ghar mein hamare baare mein pata chalte-chalte reh gaya.

Uske baad bahut kuch hua. Uske ghar par kya hua, mujhe aaj bhi poori tarah nahi pata.

Lekin mere ghar par situation bahut zyada kharab ho gayi. Main sab kuch explain nahi kar sakta.

Bas itna jaanta hoon ki woh waqt mere liye bhi aur uske liye bhi normal nahi tha.

Mere ghar ki situation itni difficult ho gayi ki mujhe 10 din ke liye ghar chhodkar Lucknow mein rehna pada.

1 Month & 12 Days Later

Ek Mahina Aur Baarah Din Baad

Uske baad meri usse kabhi baat nahi hui.

Aaj is sab ko 1 month aur 12 din ho chuke hain, aur main ye website bana raha hoon.

Itna waqt beetne ke baad bhi mere feelings kam nahi hue. Sach kahun toh mujhe aaj bhi usse pehle se bhi zyada pyaar hai.

Din kisi tarah guzar jaate hain, lekin main abhi tak move on nahi kar paaya.

Woh mujhse bahut pyaar karti thi. Meri har chhoti-badi baat ka care karti thi, galti hone par samjhati thi. Uska care mujhe bahut apna sa lagta tha.

Aaj bhi jab main aankhein band karta hoon, toh purani yaadein yaad aa jaati hain — uska tightly hug karna, mere baalon ko sehlana, aur woh saare chhote moments jo mere liye bahut important the.

Mujhe lagta hai ki jo kuch hua, usmein meri bhi zimmedari hai. Isi wajah se kabhi-kabhi mann karta hai ki bas ek baar usse message karke pooch loon — “Tu kaisi hai? Sab theek chal raha hai na?”

Aaj hum Instagram par bhi ek-doosre ko follow nahi karte.

Maine usse manane ke liye bahut kuch kiya hai. Kabhi maafi maangi, kabhi apni ego side mein rakhi, kyunki mere liye woh bahut important thi.

Aur aaj bhi main uska wait kar raha hoon.

Ek baar usne kaha tha ki agar kabhi woh mujhe chhodkar chali bhi jaaye, toh main uska wait karun — kyunki woh wapas aayegi.

Shayad isi baat ki wajah se mere andar aaj bhi ek umeed hai.

Main nahi jaanta woh kabhi wapas aayegi ya nahi.

Bas itna jaanta hoon ki jo jagah usne meri life mein banayi thi, woh mere liye aaj bhi utni hi important hai.

Why I Made This

Ye website use wapas lane ke liye nahi hai. Ye un yaadon ko sambhal kar rakhne ke liye hai jo meri life ka ek bahut bada hissa ban chuki hain.

“Some stories end without an ending. They simply become memories we never stop carrying.”
